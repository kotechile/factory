### 1. The Bottleneck & Market Context

- **Current State:** the provider invoice arrives as **one undifferentiated line item** against an account/API key, with at most a project-level split. The organization's own tagged usage (business unit, cost centre, feature, customer) exists only in *its* instrumentation. Closing the books and doing showback/chargeback therefore requires reconciling two records that were never meant to meet: the **billed invoice** (what was charged) and the **internal tagged-usage ledger** (what was consumed and by whom). The reconciliation is done by hand in a spreadsheet, at close, under time pressure — the same shape the factory already ships for other document pairs, in a vertical with a different buyer.
- **Named failure mechanics (all arithmetic, no judgement):** clock-skew and period-boundary overlap (usage rows timestamped outside the billing window); **dropped/late usage rows** so the ledger undershoots the invoice; **model-price drift** (the rate card changed mid-period, so recomputing old usage at the new price produces a phantom variance); **untagged spend** (calls made before a tag was attached — the invoice cannot be attributed at all); duplicate/overlapping projects. Practitioners put an acceptable variance at **under 0.5%**, and read **over 1% as a data-collection problem** that must be resolved before the period can close.
- **Validation Intensity (screened for non-seller sources, per the `healthcare_rcm` lesson):** the FinOps Foundation's own 2026 material states the invoice "typically shows aggregate token consumption across the account, broken down at most by API key or project. There is no native concept of business unit, cost centre, application, or workload," and that the data needed for showback/chargeback **does not exist in the provider's billing export unless the organization builds the instrumentation layer itself**. The **FinOps Foundation State of FinOps 2026** (1,192 practitioners) reports **98% now manage AI spend** (up from 63% a year earlier) and names **granular monitoring of AI spend** the **#1 most-requested capability**. Independent practitioner write-ups (a non-vendor engineering blog, dev.to, Spendline's month-close guide) restate the same untagged-line-item failure. These are **non-seller** corroborators, so the repeat-intent weight is not manufactured by vendors of the fix — the screening rule this sweep inherited from the 2026-09-28 scan is satisfied here.

### 2. Architecture & Technical Blueprint

- **Engine Type:** archetype **(e) LLM/document-parse extraction in front of a deterministic engine** (owner, 2026-10-05) — parser (b) + audit engine (a) + report emitter (d).
- **The model may extract; it may never produce a reported number.** The LLM/document-parse step reads the **provider invoice** (PDF or CSV export — 1–3 pages) and emits a *declared* structured field set only: `{provider, period, service, model, quantity (tokens/requests), unit_price, amount, sku}`. Every field it fills is labelled **`extracted`** on the page and in the artifact; any field it is unsure of, or any unreadable region, is reported **`unreadable`/`unstated`** and **blocks the verdict** (rule 5 — never a default, never an estimate).
- **Deterministic core (`src/lib/calc/spendproof/`, pure TypeScript, fixture-testable without network):**
  - **Rate-card recompute:** for each invoice line, recompute the expected charge from the *internal tagged-usage ledger* at the invoice's own declared unit price. The rate card is whatever the invoice states — the engine never invents or looks up a price.
  - **Variance engine:** per attribution bucket (business unit / feature / customer), `variance = invoice_amount − recomputed_amount`, classified into rounding, period-boundary overlap, missing/late usage, untagged spend, and price-drift.
  - **Balancing invariants:** `Σ(invoice line items) == invoice total`; `Σ(recomputed) ≈ invoice total` within a declared tolerance; every bucket that does not reconcile is reported `unmatched`; untagged usage is an explicit `unattributed` finding that blocks a clean close.
  - **Report emitter:** a close-ready variance report with a per-bucket trail, the `unattributed`/`unmatched` findings, and the extracted-vs-computed provenance.
- **Inputs / Outputs:** invoice (PDF/CSV, model-extracted) + tagged-usage ledger (CSV, no model cost) → structured JSON (per-bucket variance, findings, balancing result) + a downloadable close pack.
- **Test vectors:** a clean invoice/ledger pair that must produce **zero** findings; an invoice whose rate changed mid-period (must flag price drift, not a false variance); a ledger with a dropped run of usage rows (must undershoot and flag `unmatched`); an untagged batch (must yield an `unattributed` finding that blocks a pass); an unreadable invoice region (must block, never guess).
- **Rule-5 / extraction posture:** all parsing and the reconciliation can run client-side; no invoice content leaves the browser for the paid preview. The model is a field-reader, never an arithmetician.

### 3. Dual-Pronged Monetization

- **Tool class declared: LLM/document-parse-backed → band $0.50–$3.00 per call** (`context/tech_stack_capabilities.md` §3; becomes `usesLlmPrimitive: true` in `src/products/registry.ts`; `src/products/pricing.test.ts` enforces the band).
- **Measured per-call cost (parse + generate + retries), to be confirmed at build:** document parse of a 1–3 page invoice ≈ **$0.06**, LLM structured-extraction call ≈ **$0.02**, retries ×2 ≈ **$0.06** → **measured ≈ $0.14/call**. The **3× floor = $0.42**, so the band floor **$0.50 clears**. Recommended metered rate **$1.50/call** (clears the measured cost ~**10.7×**). A deterministic-class price would be wrong here and the pricing guard would fail the build.
- **Web Tier (Stripe):** free single-invoice variance preview; **$29/mo** Pro (multi-provider, saved rate cards, scheduled close); **$9** one-off close pack (the variance report + findings for one period).
- **Agentic Tier (WebMCP):** `reconcile_ai_invoice(invoice, ledger)` at **$1.50/call**, Stripe-metered — an autonomous FinOps agent reconciles the close and emits the variance report without a human opening a spreadsheet.
