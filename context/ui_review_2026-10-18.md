## Findings

| Severity | Domain | Finding | Evidence | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HIGH | writing / hierarchy | The directory's `h1` was "Factory Showcase" — a name for an internal artifact. A visitor could not tell what the suite is for. | rendered DOM, `/showcase` | `h1` is now "Prove the number before you act on it." with a one-sentence subhead naming the four document types; `<title>` keeps "Factory Showcase" for search |
| HIGH | hierarchy | Four product pages had **no product action above the fold**; SpendProof's only above-fold link was Billing. The tool sat 2–3 screens down a 1000–1200-word page. | above-fold action count per route | shared `<HeroActions>` (primary verb-first + one secondary) rendered by `EditorialHero`, each anchored to its calculator (`#audit` / `#check`) |
| MEDIUM | visual identity | Six directory cards were indistinguishable: same white surface, same indigo CTA, same grey category label. The per-domain horizon gradient is a 3.5px strip at the viewport edge — invisible on a card. | screenshot of `/showcase` | `PRODUCT_ACCENTS` in `signature.tsx` (fill + AA-safe ink per product), rendered as a 3px top rule, a Lucide glyph and the category label; `ProductHeader` initials tile now carries the same hue |
| MEDIUM | writing | Two filter rows both began with a control labelled "All" (category chips and the status segmented control); nothing distinguished them. | rendered DOM | visible `CATEGORY` / `STATUS` captions |
| MEDIUM | a11y | `dl` in the new proof strip contained `<dt>/<dd>/<p>` — axe `definition-list` (serious) failed the gate. | axe, `/showcase` | one `<dt>` + one `<dd>` per group, `flex-col-reverse` for the value-over-label order |
| MEDIUM | a11y | White on the raw accent (initials tile) measured 3.04:1 (#EA580C) and 3.42:1 (#059669) for 16px bold — axe flagged FacturGate and ParcelProof. | axe, both routes | the tile paints `ink`, not `accent`; measured 5.24:1 – 7.55:1 on white and on #F8FAFC |
| LOW | tap targets | 41/48 controls under 40px on the directory, 30/35 on billing. Card CTAs and the Specs toggle were 38px. | measured `getBoundingClientRect()` | card CTA + Specs toggle are `min-h-11` (44px); filter chips `min-h-8` (32px — passes WCAG 2.2 AA 24px, below the 44px AAA target) |

## What changed

- `src/app/showcase/page.tsx` — message-led hero, three-step "How every tool decides" panel, a
  derived proof strip (all four numbers computed from `activeProducts`, never literals), two CTAs.
- `src/components/editorial/signature.tsx` — `PRODUCT_ACCENTS` / `productAccent()`, `HeroActions`,
  `ProductHeader` takes `slug` for identity, `EditorialHero` takes `actions`.
- `src/components/directory-list.tsx` — accent rule + glyph per card, category captions, 44px CTAs.
- `src/app/{spendproof,caseproof,facturgate,parcelproof}/page.tsx` — `slug` for the header accent,
  `actions` in the hero, calculator wrapped in a scroll anchor. SpendProof's hero copy cut from
  ~90 words to ~48, problem-first.
- `tests/e2e/landing.spec.ts` — h1 assertion follows the new copy; snapshot re-cut and read.
